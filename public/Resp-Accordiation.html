Frequently Asked Questions

Netflix is a streaming service that offers a wide variety of award-winning TV shows, movies, anime, documentaries, and more – on thousands of internet-connected devices.

You can watch as much as you want, whenever you want, without a single ad – all for one low monthly price. New TV shows and movies are added every week!

Netflix offers several plans to fit your needs. Prices vary depending on your country and the plan you choose (Basic, Standard, or Premium).

All plans give you access to the full Netflix catalog, with no ads and the ability to cancel anytime.
